Suggest a hospital

Anyone can propose a facility. Suggestions are reviewed before they appear on the map.

Suggestions are not published automatically. An administrator verifies each facility against the HeFRA licensed register before it becomes visible. Under the Health Institutions and Facilities Act, 2011 (Act 829), a facility may not legally operate without a HeFRA licence, and directing an ambulance to an unverified address costs time that patients do not have.
