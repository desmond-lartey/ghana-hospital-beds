AT ACCRA HOSPITAL BEDS

A family looking for care should not have to guess. This map shows which hospitals in Greater Accra have beds free right now, so the decision is made before anyone gets in a car.

Hospital names, addresses and phone numbers come from public directories. Bed, ICU and oxygen figures are not public information: they appear here only when a member of staff at that hospital reports them.

Availability right now

Counted across every hospital listed on this map.

Bed counts are not yet live. Names, locations and phone numbers below come from public directories. Bed, ICU and oxygen figures appear only once a hospital reports them, so every hospital currently shows as not reported.

What are you looking for?

Pick one and the map and the list below both narrow to match.

Beds available now

Hospitals reporting at least one free bed in emergency, ICU or the general ward. Availability changes through the day, so call the number on the card before you set off.

Oxygen on site

Facilities that have confirmed a working oxygen supply. This matters most for breathing difficulty, where the nearest hospital is not always the right one to drive to.

Ambulance on site

Hospitals with their own ambulance available for transfers. Useful when a patient may need moving on to a larger facility after an initial assessment.

Every hospital listed

The full directory, including facilities that have not reported today. Their contact details are still here, so a phone call is always the next step available to you.

    ARE YOU HOSPITAL STAFF?

    Four numbers, twice a day.

    If you work on a ward in Greater Accra, you can put your hospital on this map. Reporting takes about thirty seconds and reaches families the moment you press submit.

    • Emergency, ICU and general ward counts
    • Oxygen and ambulance, yes or no
    • Correct your hospital's phone number and address
    • Accounts are approved by hand before they can change anything
