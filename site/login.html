About this site

Accra Hospital Beds is an open-source student project mapping bed availability at hospitals in Greater Accra. It is not affiliated with any hospital, bank, government agency or commercial service, and it does not impersonate one.

This sign-in is only for hospital staff who report their own ward's bed counts. Accounts are created on request and approved by hand. We never ask for payment details, identity documents, or any information beyond an email address and a password you choose.

Sign-in is handled by Supabase. The full source code is public at github.com/desmond-lartey/ghana-hospital-beds, so anyone can read exactly what this page does with what you type.

In a medical emergency in Ghana, call the National Ambulance Service on 112. This site is not an emergency service.
